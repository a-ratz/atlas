# First run: an empty Atlas and its agent

A person installed Atlas a minute ago and opens it for the first time, with no notes yet.

## Steps

1. Open Atlas. Read what the empty Home says; follow its first suggestion if it makes one.
2. Open the operator page and ask the agent: "What can you do for me here?"
3. While it answers, watch the bar above the conversation and the status pill.
4. Write a thought as a note ("Remember to look into spaced repetition") and find it again.

## Must be observable

- The empty Home tells a newcomer what to do first; nothing on it is broken or blank. A suggestion
  puts a whole question into the box, with the part to make your own selected.
- Before the agent listens, the pill says it is starting, not offline. The agent answers in the chat
  within a minute; the pill shows it working, then listening.
- "What can you do" gets a short list of what the agent does here.
- The reminder is kept and shows on Home under "Needs you".
